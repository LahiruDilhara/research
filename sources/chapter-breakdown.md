## Chapter 01: Introduction

- **1.1 Chapter Overview**
- **1.2 Problem Background**
  - What has actually happened?
- **1.3 Problem Statement**
  - More focused and supported with recent citations; justify the relevancy via latest statistics or applicable info.
  - **1.3.1 General Problem:** Implication caused by the problem at a high level.
  - **1.3.2 Specific Problem:** Discipline / domain specific shortcomings. Better to terminate by highlighting the research gap to be addressed.
- **1.4 Research Question**
  - 'Wh' form of the problem statement. Limit to one, but can have sub-optimal regions.
- **1.5 Research Motivation**
  - What paved the way for you to do this research?
- **1.6 Research Aim**
  - What do you expect after doing this research?
- **1.7 Research Objectives**
  - Must not be learning objectives common to all students who follow the module. Research objectives must be research specific. At undergrad level, we can use the following phrases to standardize the writing of the research objectives:
    - **1.7.1** To Identify...
    - **1.7.2** To analyze...
    - **1.7.3** To design / implement / develop...
    - **1.7.4** To evaluate...
- **1.8 Rich Picture of the Proposed Solution**
  - Emphasizing on the suggested workflows.
- **1.9 Resource Requirements**
  - **1.9.1 Hardware**
  - **1.9.2 Software**
- **1.10 Project Scope**
  - Can be inclusive of a small table with "In scope" and "Out scope" supported with appropriate elaborations.
- **1.11 Chapter Summary**

## Chapter 02: Literature Review

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Personal reviewing is a must; your own opinions have to be discussed against the existing literature assessed.

- **2.1 Chapter Overview**
- **2.2 Conceptual Map of the Literature**
  - This will be helpful for the reader to get a quick overview about how the literature is organized within the chapter.
- **2.3 Domain Overview**
  - Surface level but significant info (10% coverage).
- **2.4 Existing Systems / Frameworks / Designs**
  - Comparative assessment, supported via personal reflections (30% coverage).
- **2.5 Technological Analysis (60% coverage)**
  - **2.5.1 Algorithmic Analysis**
  - **2.5.2 Design Analysis**
  - **2.5.3 Workflow Analysis**
- **2.6 Reflection**
  - Justify the research gap by linking with the findings gathered from the latest literature (usually not more than 5 years older from the current year, unless associated with a fundamental theorem) assessed.
